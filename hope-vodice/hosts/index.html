About us

A family house, not a rental portfolio

We are Nada and Bernard Mikulić. Vodice is our home town, we live a few minutes from the house, and we have been taking guests here for years.

The three apartments sit on the first and second floors, each with its own balcony. We keep them simple and we keep them clean: that is the whole promise. What we add is being there. If something breaks we fix it that day. If you want to know which beach has shade in the afternoon, or where the fish is good this week, ask.

We speak Croatian, English and German.

How we work

What to expect from us

A real reply

Write to us and a person answers, usually the same day. No automated system in the middle, no agency taking a cut and a week to respond.

Flexible arrival

Check-in is from 14:00 and check-out by 10:00. If your flight lands at an awkward hour, tell us and we will do whatever the cleaning schedule allows.
