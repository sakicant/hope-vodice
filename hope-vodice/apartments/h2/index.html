The same size as H1, set up for cooking

Forty square metres on the first floor, with a double bed and a sofa that opens into a second. Where H1 has the child bed, H2 has the better kitchen: a full stove and oven rather than a hotplate.

If you plan to shop at the Vodice market and cook what you find, this is the one to take. The kamado charcoal grill outside is shared with the other two apartments.
