Booking questions

Is booking direct cheaper than Booking.com?
Yes. Booking direct skips the platform commission, so the rate you see here is the lowest we offer, and you deal with Nada and Bernard rather than a messaging system.
How do I confirm a reservation?
Send us your dates. We reply with the total and payment details for the deposit that holds the booking. Nothing is confirmed until we have written back to you, so always wait for our reply.
What if I need to cancel?
Cancel more than 30 days before arrival and the deposit comes back to you in full. Plans change and we would far rather hear about it early.
