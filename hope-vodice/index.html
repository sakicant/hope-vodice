Good to know

Common questions

What is the difference between the three apartments?
H1 and H2 are both 40 m² for up to four guests, on the first floor. H1 has a child bed and the kamado grill; H2 has a kitchen with a full stove. H3 is the big one: 70 m² on the top floor, two separate bedrooms for up to six guests, and a 33 m² private balcony.
Can we book more than one apartment?
Yes, and families often do. The three are in the same house, so a larger group can take two apartments and still eat together. Tell us the dates and how many of you there are and we will work out which combination fits.
What is in every apartment?
Air conditioning, free Wi-Fi, a full kitchen with a fridge and coffee machine, a private bathroom with a shower, hairdryer and free toiletries, a flat-screen TV, an in-room safe and a balcony. The kamado charcoal grill is shared.
Is there parking?
Ask us when you book and we will tell you exactly where to leave the car. The house is in the middle of Vodice, so most guests park once and walk everywhere.
