How a booking works here, in the order it happens.

Last updated 10 November 2026.

Making a booking

Send us the dates, the apartment and the number of guests, through the form, by email, by phone or on WhatsApp. We reply with whether it is free and what the total is. Nothing is reserved until we have written back to you, so please wait for our confirmation before booking flights.

Confirming it

A reservation is held by a deposit of 15% of the total. We send you the payment details when we confirm the dates. If the deposit does not reach us within 30 days of that message, the dates go back on the calendar.

The rest is paid on arrival. Prices are in euro and are for the whole apartment, not per person.

Cancellation

Cancel more than 30 days before your arrival date and the deposit is returned to you in full. Inside 30 days the deposit is not refunded, because at that point the dates are hard to fill again.

If we have to cancel on you, for something broken or anything else, you get everything back, and we will help you find somewhere else in Vodice.

Arriving and leaving

Check-in is from 14:00 and check-out by 10:00. We live in Vodice, so if your flight lands at an awkward hour, tell us in advance and we will do whatever the cleaning schedule allows.

Croatian law requires us to register every guest, so please bring ID or a passport for everyone staying.

What the price includes

Water, electricity, air conditioning, Wi-Fi, bed linen, towels and the final clean. Croatian tourist tax is charged separately, at the rate set by the town for that year.

Liability

We look after the apartments and keep them safe and working. We cannot take responsibility for your belongings, for accidents caused by ignoring the house rules, or for things outside our control such as weather, power cuts or road closures.

Law

Croatian law applies, and any dispute goes to the competent court in Šibenik.
