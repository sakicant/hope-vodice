Privacy policy

Short, because this website collects very little.

Last updated 10 November 2026.

Who we are

Hope Apartments, {{site.address.full}}. The hosts are Nada and Bernard Mikulić. You can reach us at {{site.email}} or on {{site.phone}}.

What this site collects

Only what you type into the booking form: your name, your email address, the dates you are asking about, the number of guests and your message. There is nothing else. The site has no advertising, no tracking pixels and no analytics script, and it sets no cookies of its own.

The calendars and rates load from plain files on this server. Reading them stores nothing about you.

What we do with it

We use it to answer you and, if you book, to arrange your stay. We do not sell it, we do not add you to a mailing list, and we do not pass it to anyone except where the law requires it, such as the guest registration every host in Croatia must file with the authorities.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not become a booking are deleted within a year. Records tied to an actual stay are kept as long as Croatian tax and tourism rules require, and then deleted.

Where it is stored

Your message reaches us as email and stays in our mailbox. The website itself keeps no database of enquiries.

Things this site loads from elsewhere

  • Google FontsServes the two typefaces. Google sees the request.
  • Google MapsThe map on the contact page. It loads only on that page.

Links to Booking.com, Tripadvisor, Facebook and WhatsApp take you to those companies, where their own privacy terms apply, not ours.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to {{site.email}} and we will do it. If you think we have handled your data badly, you can complain to the Croatian Personal Data Protection Agency (AZOP).
